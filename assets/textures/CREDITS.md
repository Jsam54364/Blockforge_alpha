# Textures

All block textures here are original, painted by `tools/paint_textures.py` in the manner of
Vintage Story's (32 x 32, seamless, fine-grained and low in contrast); none are copied from
any game or pack. Grass tops and leaves are greyscale and tinted by biome in the game; a grass
side is soil plus `<name>_overlay.png`, the greyscale grass hanging over it. Edit the PNGs
directly, or change the painter and run it again:
`python3 tools/paint_textures.py` (needs numpy and Pillow).
